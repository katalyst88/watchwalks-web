Garmin

Out now. About five minutes to your first kilometre.

Two quick steps, and your everyday walking starts carrying you up 43 km of Inca stone to Machu Picchu. For Forerunner, Fenix, Venu, Vivoactive, Epix, Instinct and other Connect IQ watches.

Get it on Connect IQ

Set up your Garmin in three steps

1

Add the Inca Trail

The Inca Trail is free on the Connect IQ store. You'll need the free Garmin Connect app with your watch paired. Open the Connect IQ Store, search Watch Walks, and download it.

Get it on Connect IQ
2

Open it on your watch

Sync your watch, then open Watch Walks once on the watch to start. On Garmin the watch only sends your steps to the trail while the app is open, so open it now and then to keep your progress up to date.

Garmin setup guide
3

Get the companion (optional)

The free phone app turns your steps into a living map, opens a story at every landmark, and gives you a medal for each trail, reading straight from your watch over Bluetooth. It is out now on both stores, and the watch walks on its own whether you install it or not.

Companion on Google Play Companion on the App Store

Need a hand?

Stuck? It's almost always one of these three.

  • The app didn't appear on my watch. Open Garmin Connect and sync again. It installs on the next sync, and the watch needs enough free space.
  • My progress looks behind. Open Watch Walks on the watch to send the latest steps, then sync the companion.
  • Still stuck? Email support@watchwalks.com and we'll sort it out.
