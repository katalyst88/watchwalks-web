Let's get you walking.

Two quick steps and your everyday steps start carrying you down the Inca Trail — it's free. For Forerunner, Fenix, Venu, Vivoactive, Epix, Instinct and other Connect IQ watches. Most people are walking in about five minutes.

Get it on Connect IQ

1

Add the Inca Trail

The Inca Trail is free on the Connect IQ store. You'll need the free Garmin Connect app with your watch paired. Open the Connect IQ Store, search Watch Walks, and download the Inca Trail.

Get it on Connect IQ
2

Open it on your watch

Sync your watch, then open Watch Walks once on the watch to start. On Garmin the watch only sends your steps to the trail while the app is open, so open it now and then to keep your progress up to date.

Garmin setup guide
3

Get the companion (optional)

The free phone app turns your steps into a living map, opens a story at every landmark, and gives you a badge for each trail — reading straight from your watch over Bluetooth.
