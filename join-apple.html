iPhone and Apple Watch

The steps Apple Health already counts, pointed at Machu Picchu.

Watch Walks for iPhone and Apple Watch is coming soon to the App Store. There is no workout to start and no GPS to drain the battery: it reads the step count your phone and watch already keep, and walks you the 43 km of the Inca Trail with it. For iPhone (iOS 16+) with Apple Watch Series 4 or later (watchOS 10+).

Coming soon to the App Store

Set up your iPhone and Apple Watch in three steps

1

Install from the App Store

Open the App Store on your iPhone, search for Watch Walks, and tap Get. It's free to install, and the Inca Trail is free to walk.

Coming soon to the App Store
2

Open it and allow Apple Health

Open Watch Walks and, when asked, allow it to read your step count from Apple Health. That's the one permission that matters, because your steps are what carry you down the trail. No account needed.

Apple setup guide
3

Add the Watch app

The Apple Watch app installs alongside the iPhone app. Open the Watch app on your iPhone if it doesn't appear automatically. A glanceable progress ring and every landmark's story, right on your wrist.

Apple setup guide

New to how it works? See the iPhone & Apple Watch questions or read the full Apple setup guide.

Need a hand?

Stuck? Start with Apple Health.

  • My Apple Watch app didn't appear. Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and install Watch Walks.
  • No steps are counting. Open Watch Walks and allow Apple Health access for step count; that's what carries you down the trail.
  • Progress looks behind. Pull to refresh in the app; it re-reads Apple Health and syncs with your watch.
  • Still stuck? Email watchwalksupport@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
