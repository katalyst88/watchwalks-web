Watch Walks for iPhone and Apple Watch is in early access, on its way to the App Store. Join the TestFlight beta and your everyday steps start carrying you down the Inca Trail today — it's free. For iPhone with Apple Watch Series 4 or later (watchOS 10+).

Beta apps for iPhone install through Apple's free TestFlight app. Install it from the App Store first — it's how you'll get Watch Walks and its updates while it's in beta.
