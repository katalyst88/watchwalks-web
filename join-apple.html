iPhone & Apple Watch

Let's get you walking.

Watch Walks for iPhone and Apple Watch is on the App Store. Install it and your everyday steps start carrying you down the Inca Trail today — it's free. For iPhone (iOS 16+) with Apple Watch Series 4 or later (watchOS 10+).

Get it on the App Store

1

Install from the App Store

Open the App Store on your iPhone, search for Watch Walks, and tap Get. It's free to install, and the Inca Trail is free to walk.

Get it on the App Store
2

Open it and allow Apple Health

Open Watch Walks and, when asked, allow it to read your step count from Apple Health. That's the one permission that matters — your steps are what carry you down the trail. No account needed.

Apple setup guide
3

Add the Watch app

The Apple Watch app installs alongside the iPhone app — open the Watch app on your iPhone if it doesn't appear automatically. A glanceable progress ring and every landmark's story, right on your wrist.

Apple setup guide

New to how it works? See the iPhone & Apple Watch questions or read the full Apple setup guide.
