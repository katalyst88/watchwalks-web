Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

Watch Walks is built around a simple promise: your data stays yours. Our apps are designed to work without collecting any personal information. This policy explains exactly what that means across Garmin, Wear OS, and Apple Watch.

The short version

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking or profiling. We never sell, rent, or share your personal data. By default everything happens on your own devices and there is no account to create. A few features are optional and only run if you choose to use them — cloud backup (which does sign you in, and is the only thing that ever leaves your phone) and buying a paid trail — and each is described below.

Who we are

Watch Walks (the "data controller" for privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA) makes and supports these apps. You can reach us any time at watchwalksupport@gmail.com.

Your step and activity data

To work out how far you've travelled along your chosen trail, Watch Walks reads your daily step and distance data from your watch. It also reads your flights-climbed / elevation-gained figure to award climbing achievements (such as reaching the height of Everest over time). Depending on your device, that reading is handled by:

  • Garmin Connect IQ on a Garmin watch;
  • Wear OS Health Services on a Wear OS watch;
  • Apple HealthKit (Apple Health) on iPhone and Apple Watch.

In every case this data is read and processed on your own device only. Your health and activity data is never sold, never used for advertising, and not shared with any third party without your consent. Your trail progress is saved in the app's local storage on your watch and phone. On Apple devices, you can review or withdraw the app's Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

The companion app

The free Watch Walks companion app for your phone (Android or iPhone) reads your trail progress from your watch over Bluetooth and stores it locally on your phone, so it can show your journey and the badges you've earned. By default it creates no account and sends your data nowhere. The optional features below run only if you turn them on.

Optional cloud backup

If you'd like your owned trails, progress, and finisher badges to sync across your devices ("buy once, sync everywhere"), you can turn on cloud backup. It uses the backup service for your platform:

  • Google Firebase on Android and Wear OS, tied to your Google account;
  • Apple iCloud on iPhone and Apple Watch, tied to your Apple Account.

Backup is optional and lives in your own account. If you turn it on, here is exactly what it holds:

  • your progress along each trail (the distance you've covered), the finisher badges you've earned, your walking-day streak, and which trails you own;
  • on Android, the email address of the Google account you sign in with, so your journey can be found again on a new phone.

That is all. Your raw step data is never backed up: only the distance it has already carried you along a trail. We store no payment details, and we never share any of it with anyone.

You can turn backup off at any time. To delete it, open the app and go to Settings → Back up your journey → Delete cloud backup: this permanently removes your backup and the account behind it. (The journey on the phone in your hand is left exactly as it is; uninstalling the app removes that.) You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Downloading a trail's map

Trail maps are downloaded once, then work entirely offline. On Android some are fetched from a public file store (GitHub's content delivery network) and the rest through Google Play; on iPhone they come from Apple's App Store. As with any download, the service delivering the file can see the request and your IP address. We send it nothing about you: no account, no identifier, and no information about your walking. Once a map is on your phone, nothing further is requested.

Buying a trail

The Inca Trail is free. Any other trail is a one-time purchase, handled entirely by the app store for your device:

  • Google Play Billing on Android and Wear OS;
  • Apple App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and Apple Watch;
  • Garmin Connect IQ on Garmin.

The store handles the payment and tells the app which trails you own. We never see your card or billing details.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. We do not set tracking cookies, run analytics, or embed advertising. Our hosting provider may keep basic, anonymised server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability, as is standard for any website; we do not use these to identify or profile you.

Children

Watch Walks is not directed at children, and because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your control and your rights

You are always in control. By default your data lives only on your own devices. If you turned on cloud backup, turning it off stops syncing, and Settings → Back up your journey → Delete cloud backup erases the backup and its account for good. On Apple devices you can withdraw Health access in the Health app at any time; on Android you can withdraw step access in your phone's app permissions. Uninstalling an app removes its stored progress from that device. Because we hold no personal profile on you, there is nothing for us to sell or profile, and you can email us with any privacy request.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the apps handle data, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact
