Wear OS and Samsung

Let's get you walking.

Watch Walks runs right on your wrist, turning your everyday steps into a journey down the Inca Trail. It's free. For Samsung Galaxy Watch, Google Pixel Watch, TicWatch, OnePlus Watch and other Wear OS 3+ watches.

Get it on Google Play

Set up your Wear OS watch in three steps

1

Install on your watch

Open the Play Store on your watch, search for Watch Walks, and tap Install. It downloads straight to your wrist. Start with the free Inca Trail.

Get it on Google Play
2

Open it and allow steps

Open Watch Walks once and allow the Physical activity permission so it can count your steps. That's it. It's standalone, with no phone pairing and no account. Just start walking.

Wear OS setup guide
3

Get the companion (optional)

The free phone app turns your steps into a living map, opens a story at every landmark, and gives you a badge for each trail you finish. Install it from Google Play on your phone.

Need a hand?

Stuck? It's almost always the step permission.

  • I can't find it on my watch. Make sure the watch is on Wi-Fi or has its phone nearby, then open the Play Store on the watch and search Watch Walks.
  • My steps aren't moving the trail. Open the app, then in system Settings → Apps → Watch Walks → Permissions, allow Physical activity.
  • "Not compatible" on my phone. Install the watch app from the watch's Play Store, and the phone companion from the phone's Play Store.
