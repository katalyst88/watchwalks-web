Story cards and real photos

This list is set by Health Connect, not by us, and can change over time. Watch Walks reads whatever step data an app writes to Health Connect. We cannot control or guarantee which apps that includes. Apple devices use Apple Health instead, and Garmin watches run the trail on the watch directly.

Companion app

The watch does the walking. The phone is where you sit and look at it.

The free companion app for Android and iPhone is the big screen for everything above: the map, the landmark stories, the medal wall. It is optional. Your trail keeps counting whether you open it or not.

  • You never have to open it. The watch counts your steps on its own. The phone app pulls your progress across over Bluetooth whenever you do open it, or when you tap Sync.
  • It is free, on both platforms, and it is not a second purchase. A trail you buy is yours on the watch and the phone, on the same store account.
  • It works with no signal. Each trail's map downloads once, so the app is just as useful in a valley in the Andes as it is on the sofa.
Built for privacy

Private by default.

No ads, no analytics, no profiling. There is no Watch Walks account to create. Your trails and medals are backed up through the iCloud or Google account you already have, so a new phone picks up where the old one left off, and Settings → Start over erases the lot.

No account needed.

Install and walk. There is no sign-up screen between you and the trail, and we never ask who you are. The only account in the picture is the Google or Apple one already on your phone, which is where your trails and medals are backed up so a new phone finds them.

Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Short, honest answers. If yours isn't here, send us a message and a real person will reply. There are more in the full questions page.

Is it a subscription?
No. The Inca Trail is free, and every other trail is a one-time purchase. Buy a trail once and it's yours to keep, with no recurring charge.
Do I need to carry my phone, or keep the app open?
No. On Garmin and Wear OS the watch app counts on its own, so just open it now and then. On Apple Watch it reads your steps from Apple Health automatically.
Does it work offline on the trail?
Yes. Each trail's map downloads once, then needs no signal. Your steps keep counting whether you're online or not.
Which watches work?
Garmin (Connect IQ), Wear OS watches like Samsung Galaxy Watch and Google Pixel Watch, and Apple Watch (Series 4 and later on watchOS 10+). See the compatible devices.
Where do my steps come from?
From your watch, from Apple Health on Apple devices, or from Health Connect on Android: Fitbit, Samsung Health, Google Fit and more.
Is my data private?
Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your step data is read on your own device and stays there. Read the full privacy policy.
