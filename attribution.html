Some of what you see here is other people's work. The trails, the stories, the medals and the elevation profiles are ours. The basemaps, the landmark photographs, the two typefaces and the device frame our screenshots sit in are not, so here is who made them.

Device mockups

  • Phone frame (Google Pixel 9 Pro). Device frame from the open mockup-device-frames collection by James Jingyi (GitHub). We present screenshots of the Watch Walks companion app in it.

Maps and imagery

  • Trail maps. Vector basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors, tiles by Protomaps; terrain hillshade derived from public-domain elevation data.
  • Landmark photos in the companion app come from Mapillary contributors, used under CC BY-SA with per-photo credit in the app.
  • Elevation. Every profile on this site is sampled from a public 30 m digital elevation model (SRTM, ASTER and Mapzen terrain tiles) along each trail's real route. The figures are ours to get wrong, not a guidebook's: where a trail's high point carries a "~", it is our reading of the ground rather than a surveyed number.

Type

  • Fraunces and Hanken Grotesk typefaces, both under the SIL Open Font License.
