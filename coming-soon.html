What to expect

Your journey, on your wrist.

  • Your steps become a trail. Live your life as normal, and your everyday steps move you along the real route of each trail.
  • A story for every landmark. Reach a milestone and unlock a short postcard from that place, so each trail becomes a journey you remember.
  • Medals and feats to collect. Every trail has its own medal, with dozens more to earn for your streak, your biggest day, and your lifetime distance.
  • Private by design. No ads, no tracking, no sign up. Your steps come from Apple Health and stay on your device.
