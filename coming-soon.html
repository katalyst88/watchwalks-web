Where to get it

On Garmin today. iPhone and Android are close.

Watch Walks is out on the Garmin Connect IQ store right now, with the Inca Trail free. The iPhone and Android apps are finished and waiting on their stores. Rather than send you to a page that does not exist yet, here is exactly where each one stands.

Garmin
Out now. Forerunner, Fenix, Venu, Vivoactive, Epix, Instinct and other Connect IQ watches. The trail runs on the watch itself, and the Inca Trail is free. Get it on Connect IQ
iPhone and Apple Watch
Not in the App Store yet. The app is built and going through Apple's review. The moment it is approved the link appears here and on the Apple page. There is nothing to sign up for and no list to join. Just check back.
Android and Wear OS
Not on Google Play yet. It is in closed testing while Google reviews it for public release. Same promise: the link lands here and on the Wear OS page the day it opens.
